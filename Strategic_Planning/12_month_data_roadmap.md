# 12-Month Data & Insights Strategic Roadmap

## Vision
Transition the organization from reactive, siloed reporting to proactive, automated decision intelligence while maintaining strict governance and cost efficiency.

| Phase | Timeline | Strategic Theme | Key Deliverables | Business Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Q1** | Months 1–3 | **Stabilize & Align** | • Audit legacy reports & retire 30% unused assets<br>• Establish executive KPI glossary<br>• Launch structured analytics intake process | Eliminate metric discrepancies across Finance, Sales, and Ops. |
| **Q2** | Months 4–6 | **Modernize & Automate** | • Migrate core reporting tables to Medallion (Silver/Gold) architecture<br>• Implement automated `dbt` data quality testing<br>• Automate top 5 recurring weekly executive packs | Reduce manual analyst prep time by 15+ hours/week. |
| **Q3** | Months 7–9 | **Scale Self-Service** | • Deploy certified Gold semantic models to Power BI<br>• Run Data Champions enablement program across business units | Achieve 70%+ self-service adoption for operational queries. |
| **Q4** | Months 10–12 | **Predictive & Proactive** | • Deploy automated statistical anomaly detection on core revenue/churn KPIs<br>• Launch customer retention propensity scoring | Shift leadership from historical hindsight to early intervention. |

## Team Capacity Allocation Model
* **50% Strategic Initiatives**: Roadmap delivery, new data products, cross-functional projects.
* **30% Business-As-Usual (BAU) & Insights**: Deep-dive commercial analysis and executive decision support.
* **20% Platform & Technical Debt**: Pipeline optimization, documentation, upskilling, and governance.
